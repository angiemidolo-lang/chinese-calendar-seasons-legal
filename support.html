FAQ

What years are supported?

2026–2036.

Does it need internet?

No for core calendar functionality. Calculations and cultural content are available offline.

Does it show official Chinese public-holiday vacation arrangements?

No. The app identifies festivals and traditional dates but does not provide government workday / holiday-adjustment schedules.

Does farming information replace agricultural advice?

No. Farming notes are traditional, general seasonal cultural references. Actual timing varies by region, variety, and climate.

Does Plum Rain mean a weather forecast?

No. Plum Rain markers in the app are traditional seasonal calendrical markers, not modern weather prediction.

How do I change language?

Open Settings → Language. First launch also offers a language selection screen.
